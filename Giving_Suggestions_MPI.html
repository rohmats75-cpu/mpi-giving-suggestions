Discover the expressions

Read the conversation. Look for the problem, the suggestions, and the responses.

Andi
Hi, Budi. You look worried. What's wrong?
Budi
I have an English test tomorrow, but I don't understand the lesson.
Andi
Why don't you review your notes and practice some questions?
Budi
That's a good idea! Should I study all night?
Andi
No, you shouldn't. You should get enough sleep, too.
Budi
You're right. Thanks for your suggestions!
THINK ABOUT IT

Can you spot them?

Problem: Budi does not understand the lesson and feels worried.

Giving a suggestion: “Why don't you review your notes...?”

Accepting a suggestion: “That's a good idea!”

Giving advice with should: “You should get enough sleep.”

Remember: A good suggestion is relevant, helpful, and polite. We can offer an idea, but the other person can decide whether to follow it.

Language Lab

Explore four useful functions and learn the patterns. Tap each example aloud to practise speaking.

1 · ASKING

Asking for suggestions

“What should I do?”
“Do you have any suggestions?”
“What do you think I should do?”

2 · GIVING

Giving suggestions

“You should ask your teacher.”
“Why don't you take a break?”
“How about studying together?”
“Let's practise together.”

3 · ACCEPTING

Accepting suggestions

“That's a good idea!”
“Thanks for your suggestion.”
“I'll try that.”
“That sounds helpful.”

4 · RESPONDING

Declining politely

“Thanks, but I'd rather try another way.”
“That's kind of you, but I don't think I can do that.”

Grammar patterns

ExpressionPatternExample
Shouldshould + base verb (V1)You should rest.
Ought toought to + V1You ought to ask for help.
Why don't you…?Why don't you + V1?Why don't you talk to her?
How/What about…?How/What about + verb-ing?How about studying together?
Let's…Let's + V1Let's practise together.
If I were you…If I were you, I would + V1If I were you, I would apologise.
Grammar alert!
❌ You should to study. → ✅ You should study.
❌ How about study together? → ✅ How about studying together?
